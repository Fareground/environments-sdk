## Reading

- Guide text read per session fell by a third (21.1k → 13.6k tokens) and nobody fetched `all` (8/8 → 0/8).
  Authors still fan out over ~18 section pages — the model's habit, not a missing page — but sessions that finish
  under the cap went 1/8 → 3/8 and total tokens fell 22%.
- Fidelity 26/36 → 28/36 (n = 8, within noise). Classroom kept the brief's five students in both samples (before:
  3 students in one), and treaty red lines leaked in 1 of 2 samples instead of 2 of 2. New misses after: beer_game
  s1 added a fifth "filler" role to the cost table, and beer_game s2's last write (made after a clean one, just before
  the cap) fails at runtime, so none of its checks could run.
- Writes to a clean check rose (1.5 → 2.6): with less reading up front, authors lean on `check`, which now names the
  guide part for structural errors. That is the intended loop, but it is not free.
- Cost for all 16 sessions: $0.75.
