12 briefs × 3 samples, `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, 600k-token cap per brief, SDK `e7d0978` (round 2, before
T-825, which does not change authoring). The author loop is now `fg_env.author` itself (T-823).

| reached a clean `check` | 12/12 | 36/36 | 36/36 |

"Last write" is what the harness scored until now. The saved contract is the latest one that checks clean and runs,
which is what a user of `fg-env author` receives. In the last-write column, three of the round-2 misses are a final
write that broke a contract that had worked; `fg_env.author` never saves those.

What still misses:
- **Required output names.** In beer_game s2 and sealed_tender s1 the author renamed or dropped outputs the brief
  requires.
- **The treaty red-line check (2 of 3).** A nation's announced proposal equalled its own secret red line. This is
  arguably the nation's own disclosure rather than an engine leak; the check is a text heuristic.
- **auction_house s2.** No write both checked clean and ran.
